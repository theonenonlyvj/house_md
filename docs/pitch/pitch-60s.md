# house_md — the spoken 60-second pitch

Use: voiceover spine for the 2-minute video (trim to beats), and the live opener if
the team reaches the Q&A round. ~165 words, ~60 seconds at presentation pace.

---

Everyone at this hackathon built the patient an agent. We built the doctor a council.

house_md. A doctor presents a hard case by voice. The room seats itself: specialists
chosen by the case, a skeptic, and a reimbursement seat that has already run the
patient's insurance. They argue the differential out loud, in different voices, citing
the patient's real record. A claim without a citation gets demoted to conjecture, on
screen. A specialty the council can't seat shows as an empty chair, and the chair says
so.

Here's who needs it: one in five Americans lives where the specialists aren't. Their
doctors carry the hardest cases with the least backup. We give that doctor the team.

Today the council caught a pattern scattered across eight years of chart, checked
coverage live, re-sequenced the plan around a referral gate, and wrote the confirmed
plan back to the chart as FHIR. The doctor decided every step.

Diagnosis is a team sport. Now every doctor has the team.

---

## Delivery notes

- "Everyone…built the patient an agent" is the contrast line; pause after it.
- "on screen" and "says so" are the two beats to slow down: the guardrails are the flex.
- Say "house, M.D." out loud; the styling stays lowercase in print.

## Truth gate (check before speaking)

- "caught a pattern scattered across eight years of chart" = the Moss re-rank beat.
  Requires the 3:30 golden-path run to have passed. Fallback line if it didn't:
  "Today the council argued a real differential from a real record."
- "wrote the confirmed plan back to the chart" = the finalize beat. Same gate.
  Fallback: end on "checked coverage live, before options were discussed."
- "checked coverage live" is safe: Stedi adapter integrated, 34/34 scenario sweep
  verified this morning.
