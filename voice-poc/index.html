Voice playground LOCAL — keys stay on the server

Four independent panels for poking at the voice + retrieval legs of the main build: Deepgram text-to-speech (Aura), speech-to-text (nova-3), the full Voice Agent (speech in → LLM → speech out, with a wired chart-lookup tool), and Moss semantic search over 20 synthetic record snippets. Everything here is fake-patient data (Maria Okafor, invented).

Try this first:
  1. Speak: hit ▶ — hear a voice. Swap the voice, hit it again.
  2. Transcribe: click a sample clip button (no mic needed). Then record yourself mumbling “metoprolol”.
  3. Converse: put on headphones, click a personality (try the attending 😈), Start session, allow the mic, and say “look up Maria Okafor — what’s her last A1c?”. Then edit the prompt and hit Restart.
  4. Retrieve: click a canned query, watch the scores and the ms.
