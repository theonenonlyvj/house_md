# Brief: write a jingle for "house_md"

You are writing a jingle for a hackathon project. Everything you need is in this file —
do not go looking for the repo.

## The project, in one breath

**house_md** — built in one day at the YC × Medplum Agentic Healthcare Hackathon
(Aug 1, 2026) by Vijay, Thai, Noah, and Felix. A doctor presents a tough case out loud,
and gets what most doctors never have: a full council of AI specialist peers who argue
the diagnosis against each other — out loud, in different voices — citing the patient's
actual medical record for every claim. If the case needs an expert the council doesn't
have, the chair says so instead of faking it (there's literally an empty chair on
screen). Then the twist: the council checks what the patient's insurance will actually
pay for and reshapes the care plan around reality. The doctor decides everything; the
AI just argues. Tagline material: "Diagnosis is a team sport. Most doctors don't have
the team."

## What the jingle must do

- **Length:** short — a 20–40 second sing (roughly 4–8 lines + a hook). Write the
  lyrics; a longer "full verse" version is welcome as a bonus.
- **The hook should land the name** ("house_md" — pronounced "House M.D." is fine).
- **Capture at least two of these beats** (pick what sings, don't force all):
  1. A room full of specialists arguing (many voices, one patient).
  2. Every claim backed by the chart ("cite it or it's conjecture").
  3. The empty chair — honest about what it doesn't know.
  4. Insurance reality — "a diagnosis without a plan you can afford is a term paper."
  5. The doctor decides; the council argues.
- **Tone:** clever, confident, a wink of medical-drama swagger — but never mocking
  patients or doctors. Think "smart TV theme" energy, not corporate anthem, not parody
  of any existing song.

## Hard rules

- Do NOT copy or interpolate melody/lyrics of the actual House M.D. theme ("Teardrop")
  or any existing song. Original words only.
- No real patient anything — if a patient is mentioned, keep it abstract ("the chart",
  "the case"). The project uses synthetic data only.
- Don't claim the AI diagnoses people. The line is: it argues, the doctor decides.
- Keep it clean — this may be played in the demo video or the closing pitch.

## Deliverables

1. The jingle lyrics (20–40s version) with the hook clearly marked.
2. One-line performance direction (tempo, mood, e.g. "mid-tempo swing, snappy snare,
   spoken-word verse into sung hook").
3. If your tooling can generate music/audio: also produce a style prompt (genre,
   instrumentation, BPM, vocal style) suitable for a music-generation tool — and note
   that the four team member names should NOT appear in lyrics unless asked.
4. Two alternates for the hook line, so the humans can pick.
