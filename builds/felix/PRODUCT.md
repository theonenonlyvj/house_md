# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a managing clinician presenting and resolving a difficult synthetic case in a bright conference-room setting. Hackathon judges are the secondary audience: they must understand the workflow and integration proof from a projected display without operating it.

## Product Purpose

house_md is a clinician-facing reasoning workspace where an audible specialist council argues from the patient’s real longitudinal FHIR record, coverage reality reshapes a proposed plan, and the clinician explicitly decides what becomes draft chart documentation.

## Positioning

The living differential is a shared, inspectable clinical artifact: every patient-specific claim resolves to a record resource or is visibly demoted to conjecture.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and credible. The product creates drama only through real clinical disagreement, real missing expertise, and real coverage constraints.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a legacy EHR, a generic chatbot, a sponsor-logo dashboard, a casino table, or a theatrical dark-mode AI demo. Avoid decorative healthcare imagery and autonomous-diagnosis language.

## Design Principles

1. Make the evolving differential—not chat—the center of gravity.
2. Keep clinician agency visible at every consequential transition.
3. Put provenance beside the claim it supports.
4. Make real integration activity and failure legible without derailing the case.
5. Use coverage facts to sequence care precisely, never to imply guaranteed payment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, avoid color-only state, respect reduced motion, and keep primary content and controls readable on a projector. Use plain clinical language and label all people and payer data as synthetic or test data.
